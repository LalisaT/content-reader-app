Privacy Policy - TipPulse

TipPulse respects your privacy. This policy explains how our application handles your data, local storage, community polls, embedded YouTube services, and third-party advertising.

1. Cloud-Native Real-Time Architecture & Zero Offline Disk Caching

TipPulse delivers live editorial content and vacancy opportunities directly from Google Cloud Firestore:

  • Direct Cloud Streaming: Articles, guides, live announcements, and career vacancies are retrieved in real time into transient application memory. The application does not maintain persistent local databases (such as IndexedDB, SQLite, or Service Worker disk caches) for public feeds. This ensures you always view authentic, up-to-date content without stale local caches.
  • Device UI Preferences: Minimal client-side device storage (localStorage) is used solely to store your active interface preferences (theme mode, font scaling, personal article bookmarks, and saved vacancy IDs). These preferences remain strictly on your physical device.
  • Community Polls: When participating in polls, votes are recorded anonymously to compute aggregate results. A local vote flag is saved on your device solely to display your voted state and prevent duplicate submissions. No personal profile data is collected.

2. Candidate Applications & Executive Career Privacy

When utilizing the Job Vacancy & Scholarship Sanctuary portal:

  • Confidential Application Transfer: Information provided during candidate submissions (such as your name, email, phone number, portfolio/CV URL, and executive statement) is transferred directly to the designated recruiting team or verified external employer application endpoint using encrypted TLS/HTTPS protocols.
  • Strict Non-Commercial Use: Candidate CVs, portfolio links, and recruitment inquiries are never sold, rented, or distributed to advertising networks or third-party data brokers. Candidate data is processed solely for evaluating professional candidacy.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements within the app (including adaptive banners, rewarded video ads, and occasional interstitial transitions). AdMob may automatically collect and process certain data to display relevant ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID, IDFA)
  • IP address and approximate coarse location
  • In-app diagnostic, crash, and ad performance metrics

For more details on how Google processes ad data, visit Google's Advertising & Privacy Terms.

4. YouTube API Services & Embedded Multimedia

TipPulse includes embedded audio and multimedia tracks (such as ambient focus, sleep soundscapes, and educational video guides) using official YouTube API services and embedded players:

  • By using and accessing embedded YouTube content within TipPulse, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • YouTube and Google may collect usage data, cookies, and device information in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Compliance: TipPulse complies strictly with the YouTube API Developer Policies. We do not download, rip, or convert YouTube streams, nor do we provide background or screen-off playback.

5. User Rights & Choices

  • EEA/UK Residents (GDPR): You can manage your personalized ad consent preferences at any time through the in-app consent settings.
  • California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): You can opt out of interest-based advertising by adjusting the privacy settings on your mobile device (e.g., "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android or "Ask App not to Track" on iOS).

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

TipPulse is not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be reflected directly within the application alongside an updated "Last Updated" date.
