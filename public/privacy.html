Privacy Policy for TipPulse

Last Updated: October 2026

TipPulse ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information, offline storage, embedded multimedia, community polls, and third-party advertising services.

1. Information We Collect & Local Storage

TipPulse operates on an offline-first, privacy-respecting architecture:

  • Local Storage: Your bookmarks, reading history, custom theme settings, and font preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Community Polls: When participating in interactive community polls, votes are recorded anonymously to compute collective percentage results. A local vote flag is saved on your device solely to display your voted state and prevent duplicate submissions. We do not collect or store personal profiles, names, or emails for voting.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not require you to create an account with personal details like your telephone number or physical address.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

TipPulse uses Google AdMob to display banner ads, rewarded video ads, and interstitial transitions to support free content delivery:

  • Google AdMob may collect and process device advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID, IDFA), IP address, and coarse diagnostic metrics in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.
  • Users can manage ad personalization preferences in their device system settings or via the in-app consent preferences (GDPR/CCPA).

3. YouTube API Services & Embedded Multimedia

TipPulse features embedded audio, ambient soundscapes, and educational guides using official YouTube API Services and embedded players:

  • By using YouTube-enabled media features in TipPulse, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Google and YouTube process usage data in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Compliance: TipPulse complies with all YouTube API Developer Policies. We do not download, stream-rip, or convert YouTube videos or audio, nor do we support background/screen-off playback.

4. Offline Storage & Data Security

All your saved articles, customized settings, and preferences are stored securely on your local device storage. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data with any third parties.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

TipPulse does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). Our content is educational and intended for general audiences.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
